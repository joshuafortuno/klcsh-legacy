About KLCSH Legacy

A friendly Minecraft Bedrock survival server, run and funded by the people who play on it.

The server

KLCSH Legacy is a Minecraft Bedrock Edition survival multiplayer server, kept up to date with the latest official release. It's hosted through Apex Minecraft Hosting.
